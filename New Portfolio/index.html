Academic Research Spotlight

Published IEEE Research Paper

IEEE Conference Accepted for Publication PyTorch MiDaS Monocular Depth LBP Texture CASIA-FASD

"A Data Fusion-Based Two-Stage Cascading Framework for Multi-Modal Face Anti-Spoofing Using Monocular Depth Estimation"

Author: Hamiz Pathan et al. • Shah and Anchor Kutchhi Engineering College

Abstract: Physical presentation attacks (such as print photos, digital replays, and 3D silicone masks) pose severe vulnerabilities to commercial biometric authentication systems. This research proposes a novel multimodal face anti-spoofing framework that integrates 2D RGB features, monocular depth estimation maps generated via MiDaS, and Local Binary Pattern (LBP) micro-texture descriptors. By implementing a two-stage cascading architecture with feature-level data fusion, the model drastically eliminates false acceptances while retaining high validation accuracy on the CASIA-FASD benchmark dataset.

Research System Architecture Pipeline

Two-Stage Cascading Fusion Framework
Step 01
Multi-Modal Input
Captures live RGB face frames and extracts aligned facial ROIs.
Step 02
Depth & Texture Maps
Estimates monocular depth via MiDaS and generates LBP micro-textures.
Step 03
Feature-Level Fusion
PyTorch deep convolutional encoders merge spatial, depth & texture tensors.
Step 04
Cascading Classifier
2-Stage validation produces binary decision: Real vs. Spoof presentation.
